Privacy

FlameOne collects nothing, and stores nothing.

There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no third-party SDK in it. It makes no network requests of any kind — there is no networking code in the application to make one. Nothing you type into it, and nothing it computes, is transmitted anywhere.

What is stored, and where

The application writes no settings and keeps no history. Every value you enter lives only for as long as the window is open; close it and nothing of it remains. There is no document format, no library, and no recent-files list, because there is nothing to save.

Two things do touch your disk, and both are your doing:

  • Files you export. When you export results as CSV, or a chart as PNG or PDF, the system asks you where to put it and the application writes it there. It never writes anywhere you did not choose.
  • Window position. On a Mac the operating system remembers where you left the window, as it does for every application. That is written by macOS, not by FlameOne, and it stays on your machine.

The privacy manifest

The application ships a privacy manifest declaring no tracking, no tracking domains, no collected data types and no use of any API that requires a stated reason. That declaration was not written from memory: it was checked against the shipping binary’s symbol table, which contains none of the corresponding symbols.

Buying

FlameOne is sold through the App Store. Apple is the seller: it takes the payment and holds the receipt, under Apple’s privacy policy and not this one. We receive no personal information from that transaction — not your name, not your email address, not your country. We do not know who has bought a copy, and there is nothing for us to sell or share.

If you write to us for support, we will have whatever you put in that message. We use it to answer you and for nothing else.
